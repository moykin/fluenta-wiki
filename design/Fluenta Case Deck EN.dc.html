01 · Overview

Context and role

The audience is adults at B1–B2 who already read articles and books but cannot speak. Classic drills give rules and exercises; reading with translation gives comprehension without speech.

Fluenta joins three habits into one loop: reading with instant translation, retelling out loud to an AI tutor, and reviewing words already met in your own texts.

My role: research and the funnel model, information architecture, interface across all platforms, the design system, growth pages and store assets.

02 · Problem

Translation, but no language

“I understand when I read. I can’t say it”
The line every adult B1–B2 learner repeats — the project started here
Gap 1
Translation lives away from the text
A trip to the dictionary tab costs attention and breaks reading.
Gap 2
Words never come back
Hand-written notes get reviewed once. The deck is not tied to what was read.
Gap 3
Nowhere to speak
Speaking needs a partner and some nerve, so it gets postponed.

06 · Key decisions

Key interface decisions

Reader, word card, tutor and review — the four places where the loop becomes tangible.
Reader

Word → translation without leaving the text

1
Hover — the word card
Translation, part of speech and pronunciation appear next to the word. Keyboard path: Tab, then S.
2
Click — the word joins the deck
The amber highlight stays in the text: you see what you are learning without a separate list.
3
Select a phrase — get the breakdown
The panel explains why the phrase translates this way: tense, aspect, idiom, word order.
Reading progress, type size and the bookmark live in one thin bar, so the text stays the main object on screen.
Word card

Translation, form and pronunciation in one card

272 px wide — everything needed, nothing that distracts
01
Pronunciation and phonetics. Normal speed and 0.5× for hard words; British or American accent.
02
Part of speech and base form. “climbed → climb, Past Simple” — grammar explained at the moment of the question.
03
Mnemonic image. A thin line drawing instead of a second definition — faster to recognise.
04
One action — save. No forms: a click adds the word to the deck, a second click removes it.
Post-review iteration: to make the pronunciation button clickable, the popover became hover-safe — it stays put while the cursor travels from word to button.
AI tutor

Retelling out loud, mistakes explained

Speech is visible on screen
Transcription runs live and mistakes are highlighted gently — amber, not red.
A peek costs points
“Peek at the text” is available but lowers the retelling score — an honest price instead of a ban.
The mic is explained first
The −57% drop is fixed with copy before the system prompt: why access is needed and what happens to the recording.
Review

Cards made of your own texts

Cloze in context
A phrase from the text you read, with the form removed.
Translate into English
A native phrase into English, checking the key construction.
Mistake from a retelling
“he go → he went” — what you said out loud yesterday.
Offline session
Progress is kept on the device and syncs when the network returns.
Four ratings — Again / Hard / Good / Easy — set the interval: keys 1–4 on desktop, large tap zones on the phone.
Video

Words and meaning out of a video

1
Transcript with timecodes
Any line can be translated, saved as a phrase or replayed in the player.
2
A digest instead of notes
The video in five points — material to retell, not to read.
3
Words — with audio, into the deck
Only the words you cannot retell without; each one has pronunciation and a single click to a card.
Library

Pick a text by level, not by genre

A2 — C1 89% known 3 min read
Three labels per card
Level, reading time and share of known words — the decision takes a second.
Your own text
A link or a paste — the material becomes what the person already reads.
Free limit, stated upfront
The limit is shown on the card in advance, not at the moment of the tap.
Responsive

Mobile, responsive and offline

375 / 768 / 1280
Three widths were checked on live pages, not mockups: the side panel becomes a bottom sheet, the toolbar collapses into one row.
Gestures instead of hover
A tap opens the word, a long press and drag selects a phrase — the same action, a different input.
Offline by default
Review needs no network: the status is stated honestly and syncing waits for the connection.
