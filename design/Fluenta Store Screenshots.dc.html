Translation & grammar
“The old lamp flickered twice before it went out.”
TRANSLATION
Старая лампа дважды мигнула, прежде чем погасла.
WHY THIS TRANSLATION
1
went out is a phrasal verb — with lights it means “stopped burning”: погасла, not «вышла».
2
before it went out — two past events in sequence take plain Past Simple, no perfect tense needed.
Past Simple flickered → мигнула went out → погасла
